A church management system is software that helps you keep track of the people in your church and organise church life. It's sometimes called a ChMS.

## What a church management system can do

Most church management systems can help you:

- keep contact details in one secure place
- organise rotas, and let volunteers swap or confirm
- manage small groups and their members
- send emails and texts to groups of people
- take event bookings and registrations
- record giving and Gift Aid declarations
- check children in and out of groups

Some also let members update their own details and see their rotas through an app.

## Do you need one?

A small church can often manage well with shared spreadsheets and a calendar. A church management system usually becomes worthwhile when:

- several people need to see or update the same information
- rotas take a lot of time to organise
- you have several groups, ministries or services to coordinate
- contact details are spread across different lists and phones

If you're not there yet, keep your current tools tidy and secure, and review this again in a year.

## What to look for

Use the questions in [choose and change your systems](/guidance/choosing-systems/), and also check:

- how volunteers will use it, including on their phones
- whether you can set what each person can see, so that leaders only see their own groups
- whether it can record consent and contact preferences
- how it handles children's information and safeguarding
- what it costs as your church grows, as many systems charge by the number of people

Ask for a free trial and test it with real tasks, such as building next month's rota.

## Handle personal data carefully

A church management system will hold a lot of personal data, and much of it may be [special category data](/guidance/data-protection/). Before you choose one:

- check where the provider stores data, and how it protects it
- read the provider's data processing terms
- update your privacy notice to explain how you use the system
- decide what information you really need, and don't collect more

Don't add pastoral notes or safeguarding concerns unless the system is designed for them and access is tightly controlled.
