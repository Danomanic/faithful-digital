Churches often share news in many places at once: notice sheets, emails, social media, group chats and announcements. This can mean people miss things or feel overwhelmed.

## Choose your main channels

Decide on a small number of main channels, and tell people where to look. For example:

- a weekly email or printed notice sheet for church news
- your website for service times and events
- one social media account for people outside the church

Use the same information in each place, so people don't get mixed messages.

## Weekly news

A weekly email or notice sheet works well for most churches. Keep it short and put the most important items first. Give the date and time for every event, and say who to contact.

Read [send emails and texts within the rules](/guidance/emails-and-texts/) before you set up a mailing list, and our [comparison of email newsletter tools](/compare/email-newsletters/).

## Social media

Social media is mostly useful for reaching people outside your church, and for sharing what church life is like.

- create accounts in the church's name, using a church email address
- give at least 2 trusted people access, and use 2-step verification
- agree who can post, and what kind of content you'll share
- get consent before posting photos of people, and don't name children
- decide how you'll respond to comments and messages, and how quickly

You don't need to be on every platform. Choose one or two where the people you want to reach already spend time.

## Group chats

Group messaging apps are quick and popular, but they need care.

- ask people before you add them to a group
- remember that everyone in the group can see each other's phone numbers
- appoint at least 2 administrators for each group
- agree simple rules, such as no personal or pastoral details in large groups
- close or archive groups that are no longer needed
- follow your [safeguarding policy](/guidance/safeguarding-online/) for any group that includes young people

For important announcements, use your main channels as well. Not everyone uses group chats.

## Pastoral and prayer information

Take great care with prayer requests and pastoral news. Before you share anything, ask the person concerned what they're happy for others to know, and who should be told.

Don't share health or personal details in large groups or public posts.
