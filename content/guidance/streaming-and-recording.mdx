Streaming or recording services can help people who can't be there to stay connected. It doesn't need expensive equipment, but it does need the right licences and good safeguarding.

## Decide what you need

Ask who you're doing it for. Many churches find that a simple audio or video recording of the sermon, shared later, meets most needs. A full livestream takes more people and more equipment.

| Option | Good for | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Audio recording of the sermon | People who want to catch up | Low |
| Video recording, shared later | People who are housebound or away | Medium |
| Livestream of the whole service | People who want to worship at the same time | High |

## Check you have the right licences

Music and words are protected by copyright. Most UK churches use licences from CCLI.

- a Church Copyright Licence covers projecting and printing words and music for worship songs and hymns
- a Streaming Licence, added to your Church Copyright Licence, covers including those songs in a stream or recording on platforms such as YouTube and Facebook
- a Streaming Plus Licence also covers using recorded music, such as backing tracks, in a stream

Other platforms, such as video calls or your own website, may need other licences. Check with CCLI or your denomination before you start.

Playing recorded music in church, for example before a service, may need separate licences from PRS for Music and PPL.

Licence terms last checked on 18 November 2026.

## Keep people safe

Follow your church's [safeguarding guidance for livestreams](/guidance/safeguarding-online/#livestreams-and-recordings). In particular:

- tell everyone that the service is being streamed or recorded, with clear signs
- show people where they can sit to stay out of view
- point cameras at the front, not at the congregation
- get written permission from parents before children appear on camera
- stop streaming during sensitive moments, such as prayer ministry

## Keep it simple

You can start with:

- a smartphone or tablet on a stand
- a microphone connected to your sound desk, as built-in microphones often pick up echo
- a reliable internet connection, if you're streaming live

Test everything before the service, and write simple step-by-step instructions so that more than one person can run it.

## Make it accessible

Turn on captions, and check automatic captions for important mistakes. Put service details and a link in the same place each week, so people can find it easily.
